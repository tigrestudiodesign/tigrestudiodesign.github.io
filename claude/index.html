BUILD 01 · FLAGSHIP

MY JOB SEARCH COMMAND CENTER

LIVE · PRIVATE TO MERUNNING DAILY

A live dashboard plus a scheduled Claude agent that runs four times a day. I designed the process; Claude does the legwork and I approve.

  • Find: searches job boards and scores each role honestly against my real experience.
  • Tailor: drafts a resume and cover letter PDF for each role. Every claim is true or it comes out.
  • Apply: fills applications in my browser, stopping for anything I must do myself: passwords, CAPTCHAs, legal signatures.
  • Track: reads my inbox for replies and logs confirmations, rejections and interviews.
  • Learn: a tracker page shows response and interview rates by direction, so I spend time where it works.

THE PROPOSAL
