1Reading the brief brick by brick

Your six asks.
My six answers.

Each card is one responsibility from the posting, with the work that answers it.

Envision breakthrough play

Odd ideas, taken seriously

At Nike Innovation my job was the idea nobody had made yet: knit you could print on, shoes printed from data, a sensory sole for the mind.

Nike Milestone Award (2014) for a pad-printing breakthrough on knit.
Lead multidisciplinary teams

Design, engineering, factory, one table

I led and mentored a documentation team across the US, South Korea and Taiwan, and ran hardware programs with mechanical, electrical and software teams.

Nike × Hyperice Hyperboot and Air Zoom Hyperslide: I wrote the manuals factories built from.
Storytelling & transparency

Make the complex obvious

My career was visual manuals: turning engineering into pictures anyone on a line can follow. Same skill, different audience: a 9-year-old or a VP.

Years of visual manuals for products brought to market; a free Grasshopper guide told entirely through bricks.
Hybrid ideation

Hands first, code second

I prototype physically and digitally: material trials and tooling in factories, then behaviour prototypes in code and AI pipelines that cut concept time in half.

BASECAMP and the parametric keypad: simulated circuits and firmware before any PCB.
Kids' voice

Designed for small players

Two of my games teach: Spell Climb says a word out loud and rewards spelling with avatar gear; Decimal Voyage turns a math chapter into a sea voyage.

Honest note: I haven't run formal co-creation research. I'd love to learn it from your team.
Playful culture

Share everything, in two languages

I publish free learning guides and glossaries in English and Spanish, and I build in public on my studio site.

Grasshopper, Brick by Brick / Ladrillo a Ladrillo.
