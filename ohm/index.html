OHM-01

ZERO RESISTANCE.

Om, the breath. Ohm, the unit of resistance. A women's puffer high-top built like soft hardware: tuned with a heel dial, cinched with a bungee, gripped by a parametric lug sole. Cosy enough for the studio, ready for a short trail.

THREE MOODS, ONE PAIR

PUNCH

Hot-orange puffer, violet counter and toe, a yellow core glowing through the perforations and yellow lugs underneath. The loud one.

ANATOMY

What every part is doing
01 / COLLAR

PADDED, GATHERED OPENING

A tilted oval, 32 cm around, lower at the front so the foot slides in. A padded roll of outer fabric is gathered by elastic, with fleece lining inside.

02 / CINCH

BUNGEE + CORD LOCK

A second gathered channel sits lower on the shaft. A bungee exits at the instep into a cord lock, so she can seal out cold or open it right up.

03 / FIT

HEEL TUNE DIAL

A knurled, synth-style dial on an anodised bezel sets heel hold. One twist, no laces.

04 / UPPER

QUILTED RIPSTOP PUFFER

Stitched channels with soft wrinkles, a satin-coated ripstop shell and a matte fleece lining: lululemon's cosy bootie DNA, kept on purpose.

05 / COUNTER

SOFT-TOUCH HEEL CUP

A one-colour TPU counter with a rolled lip carries the OHM lockup as a clean print, plus a heel pull loop above it.

06 / MIDSOLE

GRADED PERFORATION

393 domed holes on a hex pitch, big under the heel for cushioning, shrinking toward the toe, with a glossy coloured core showing through.

07 / OUTSOLE

PARAMETRIC HIKE LUGS

Triangular lugs on a hex lattice. Heel lugs point forward to brake on descents, forefoot lugs point back for climbing, and edge lugs are bigger. Two flex grooves let the sole bend.

PROCESS & ITERATIONS

Seven rounds, each one a response
  1. ROUND 01 · LOW-TOP "SIGNAL"

    TAKE THE ELECTRONICS LITERALLY

    Brief: comfortable, cool, techy, inspired by modern electronics, still lululemon.

    • Mapped the references part by part: synth knobs became the tune dial, chassis cut-outs the heel frame, speaker grilles the vents, patch cables the lacing.
    • Exposed screws, micro-type, a barcode and a SLIP / LOCK toggle.

    Learned: too much gadget. It read as a device, not a shoe she'd want.

  2. ROUND 02 · HIGH-TOP, PUNCHIER

    FEWER, BIGGER MOVES

    Feedback: make it a high-top, less techy, more punchy. Lose the air-like windows.

    • Raised it into a quilted puffer high-top and stripped the cable, screws, grilles and small type.
    • Bold colour-blocking, an oversized dial and a big wordmark.
    • Replaced the air-style midsole windows with a 3D Voronoi groove pattern.

    Learned: punch comes from a few confident shapes, not more parts.

  3. ROUND 03 · +25% CMF & MODELLING

    MAKE THE MATERIALS BELIEVABLE

    Feedback: push the CMF and the modelling further.

    • Modelling: stitch lines and soft wrinkles on the puffs, a rolled lip on the counter, a graded and pillowed Voronoi, a 40% finer mesh.
    • CMF: satin ripstop, fleece lining, soft-touch TPU, porous foam, a glossy candy core and an anodised dial.
    • Moved the logo from 3D text to a printed decal, which fixed the garbled lettering.
  4. ROUND 04 · SOLE REFINED

    FROM CRACKED MUD TO MOULDED

    Feedback: the sole looks crude. Refine the texture (with material samples attached).

    • Took the cue from the perforated helmet sample: a hex field of round holes, graded big at the heel to small at the toe and fading at the edges.
    • Each hole is a capsule cut along the surface normal down to a coloured core, with a filleted rim.
    • Finer foam pore and topographic line rubber.
  5. ROUND 04.1 · HEEL FIX

    DEBUG THE GEOMETRY, NOT THE RENDER

    Feedback: the finish at the heel looks weird.

    • Cause: the hole rows start and end at the heel, so holes landed on top of each other and fused into a blob.
    • Fix: overlap pruning with a minimum web between holes, plus wider hex spacing so the pattern stays regular.
    • The colour line was set per vertex and came out ragged. It's now an analytic curve in the shader, and the midsole mesh is 35% finer.
  6. ROUND 05 · OUTSOLE + LOCKUP

    DESIGN THE SOLE FOR A SHORT HIKE

    Feedback: widen the collar, fix the OHM branding, make the outsole parametric and trail-ready.

    • Lug field rules: triangle lugs on an 11.2 mm hex pitch, heel lugs braking, forefoot lugs propelling, edge lugs about 25% bigger and splayed outward.
    • Two flex grooves, a 4.2 mm lug height (2.8 mm under the arch) and tapered sides so mud sheds.
    • A clean OHM over lululemon lockup in place of the warped 3D letters.
  7. ROUND 06 · THE COLLAR, RESEARCHED

    BUILD IT THE WAY BOOTIES ARE BUILT

    Feedback: really fix the collar. Research this type of shoe.

    • Research: puffer booties use an elastic gathered opening, often a second gather lower down, visible fleece lining, a drawcord with a toggle and a pull loop. Leg openings run about 28–34 cm.
    • Found the root bug: the shaft "flare" had the wrong sign, so the shaft narrowed toward the ankle.
    • Rebuilt it: a tilted plane-cut opening (32 cm), a padded ruched roll, a cinch channel, a bungee with cord lock, and a sewn-in pull loop.

    Learned: when a shape keeps fighting you, check the maths before the materials.

  8. ROUND 07 · PAIR + COLOURWAYS

    LEFT, RIGHT, THREE MOODS

    • The right shoe is a true mirror of the left. Its decal mapping is reversed so the lockup still reads correctly on both.
    • Three colourways from one model: Punch, Obsidian and Manga.

THE PIPELINE

No viewport. Every change is code.

SIGNED DISTANCE FIELDS

Every part is a function in Python: footprint, last profile, puffs, collar, counter, perforation and lugs, joined with smooth blends.

MARCHING CUBES

Fields are meshed with scikit-image at 0.5 mm voxels, plus a 0.32 mm midsole pass. A KD-tree keeps 393 hole cuts fast.

RULES AS PARAMETERS

Hole grading, lug direction, edge growth, flex grooves and the 32 cm opening are all numbers you can tune and re-run.

HEADLESS BLENDER

bpy builds the materials, decals, hardware and studio from a script, then Cycles renders with AgX Punchy.

COLOURWAYS AS DATA

Each colourway is a small table of hex values, so a new one is a few lines and a re-render.
